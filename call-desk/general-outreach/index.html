Storytelling Call Desk

Nonprofit leaders at the accounts we cleared for outreach: executive directors, marketing and comms heads, development heads. Each card has a script written for that person, built from 325 recorded calls (Aug 18 to Nov 18) and the buyer personas.

Who we are, one way

"Happy Productions. We run AreYouHappy, about 15 million people a month." Same name, same number, every call.

Reason, then one question

One sentence on why them, then ask. Monologues before a question cut calls short in every window of recordings.

Lead with reach

"Keep your team, we add an audience you don't have." "Extra pair of hands" never booked a meeting; reach made people lean in.

One ask after a no

Asking again after a clear no never worked: 0 for 18 in November alone. Take the email, ask what to put in it, set the follow-up.
